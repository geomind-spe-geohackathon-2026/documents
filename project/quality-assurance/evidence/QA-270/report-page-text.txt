Geothermal heating and cooling plant — design and verified results

Design design/v1 (project plant version 1, HackathonPlant), verified result result/9b0db55da13a4a5b: whole year 2027 on case hackathon_2026, 365 steps, 0 failed steps, gemini's verdict feasible, 15/15 requirement checks passed. Design

design/v1, family HeatingAbsorptionCoolingPlant (project plant HackathonPlant, version 1):

Two doublets, d0 and d1, each 2500 m deep with a 300 m3/h nominal flow (the case's site.reservoir.reference_depth is 2500 m; the plant's search range for depth is 2000–3500 m and for flow 50–300 m3/h). Brine leaves both doublets at 87.0 degC at the sandface.
Brine train: production mixer → degasser → desander → inhibitor dosing (10 ppm, product concentration 0.3) → counterflow heat exchanger (1500000.0 W/K) → heat pump source → injection splitter back to both doublets (full reinjection).
Heating: the heat exchanger cools the brine to the heat pump source (source_in 63.12472836477747–68.13080649070497 degC); the heat pump (15 MW, heating mode, Carnot model, carnot_fraction 0.5, minimum source outlet 47.0 degC) lifts it to the heating grid. A 30 MW gas boiler (efficiency 0.9) sits in series after the heat pump as peak backup and brings the water to the case's 100 degC heating supply. The heating supply is then split by loads: one branch to the heating grid, one to the chiller's generator.
Cooling: a 10 MW absorption chiller (thermal COP 0.7, minimum driving temperature 80.0 degC, minimum driving difference 10.0 K) makes the 20 degC chilled water from the case's cooling grid (return 25 degC). Its generator is fed from the heating supply after the heat pump — the chiller is driven by heat-pump heat, not by the boiler: boiler_heat is 253.9 MWh over the year against cold_delivered 23743 MWh, and the chiller's generator_in enthalpy flow peaks at 133.26785714285714 MW. Rejection is by a 40 MW dry cooler (cooler_type dry, approach 5.0 K, setpoint 15 degC, specific fan power 0.02).
Gas and electricity: gas co-produced by the reservoir goes to gas makeup, then to the boiler; the excess goes to the flare (combustion efficiency 0.98). Electricity from the grid drives the doublets, the heat pump, the chiller and the dry cooler. Backup policy is peak; dispatch is least_cost; outages are placed at the lowest demand.
Schematic

Two doublets (d0, d1) at 2500 m and 300 m3/h each → production mixer → degasser → desander → inhibitor dosing → counterflow heat exchanger (1500000.0 W/K) → heat pump (15 MW) → injection splitter → back to d0 and d1.

Heating water: heating-grid return (60 degC) → heat exchanger cold side → heat pump sink → boiler (30 MW) → loads splitter → (a) heating grid supply (100 degC), (b) absorption chiller generator (10 MW, thermal COP 0.7) → back to the heating return.

Chilled water: cooling-grid return (25 degC) → chiller chilled side → cooling grid supply (20 degC). Chiller cooling water (800 kg/s, 15 degC) → dry cooler (40 MW) → back to the chiller.

Gas: degasser gas → gas makeup ← gas grid; makeup fuel → boiler; makeup excess → flare. Flue gas of boiler and flare and their losses → atmosphere. Electricity grid → d0, d1, heat pump, chiller, dry cooler. Atmosphere → dry cooler air.

Decisions
Family HeatingAbsorptionCoolingPlant. Two doublets supply brine; a heat exchanger plus a 15 MW heat pump reach the 100 degC heating supply, with a 30 MW gas boiler in series as peak backup; cooling is made by a 10 MW absorption chiller driven by heat-pump heat and rejected by a 40 MW dry cooler. Reason: the case asks for 100 degC heating and 20 degC cooling from one geothermal source, and an absorption chiller lets the same heat-pump heat serve both services. No study ranks this family against others (see Open points).
Two doublets at 2500 m and 300 m3/h each. 2500 m is the case's site.reservoir.reference_depth; 300 m3/h is the top of the plant's flow range and the case's production_flow_range maximum. Two doublets are within the case's doublet_count limit of 1–3 and are what carries the 30.0 MW heat peak.
How the 100 degC supply is reached: the heat exchanger takes the brine down to the heat pump source (63.12472836477747–68.13080649070497 degC), the heat pump lifts it (temperature lift 44.28914680382667–50.88935352923272 degC, COP 3.666287485708075–4.172979254866524), and the boiler trims the last few K on 14 days (2027-01-06 to 2027-02-01), contributing 253.9 MWh of the 111670 MWh delivered.
How cooling is made: the 10 MW absorption chiller (thermal COP 0.7) is fed from the heating supply after the heat pump, not from the boiler — its generator_in temperature is 100.0 degC and its driving temperature difference 66.0495193382518–97.0 degC, and it is active 219 days. The boiler's 253.9 MWh cannot account for 23743 MWh of cold; the chiller is driven by heat-pump heat.
Backup policy peak (the boiler covers the whole heating peak with the doublets off) and dispatch least_cost are the plant file's settings; they are what the verified run used.
Agent assumptions to overrule if you wish: A_unc_samples = 200 draws for the uncertainty study (chosen by the economist; enough draws for stable p5/p50/p95 on the case's declared uncertainties) and A_unc_seed = 42 (chosen for a reproducible draw set). Both are unconfirmed.
Approved case changes: none — case_changes is empty, the case is the one the user gave.
Studies cited: study/v1_sens_permeability_gradient (sensitivity, current) and study/v1_unc_permeability_gradient (uncertainty, current, summary unreadable). Both ran on the state's case with design/v1; neither is stale.
