Executive summary

The site must be supplied with 30.0 MW peak heat at 100/60 degC and 10.0 MW peak cold at 20/25 degC over the whole year 2027-01-01 to 2028-01-01, from a 2500 m reservoir at 200 mD and a 30 K/km gradient (read_case).

The recommended design is the project's plant as it stands, design/v2, family HeatingAbsorptionCoolingPlant: two geothermal doublets (d0, d1) at 2500 m, 300 m3/h each, a 1500000.0 W/K brine/water heat exchanger, a 30 MW peak gas boiler for the 100 degC heating supply, a 10 MW absorption chiller driven by 100 degC water for the 20 degC cooling supply, a 40 MW dry air cooler rejecting the chiller's heat, and full reinjection.

It delivers 111670.0 MWh of heat and 23743.0 MWh of cold with heating and cooling coverage of 1.0 each, and gemini's verdict is feasible with 0 failed steps and 15 of 15 requirement checks passed (result/5d243b9525aa0719).

Key numbers (EUR, 2015 prices, 5 % discount, 25 yr): capex 23.96 MEUR, opex 4.978 MEUR/yr, LCOH 47.81 EUR/MWh, LCOC 56.21 EUR/MWh, LCTS 49.28 EUR/MWh, CO2 20.96 kt over the run.

The largest open points are that no alternative family was simulated and no uncertainty or sensitivity study was run in this project: the result is deterministic, and the workflow criteria that ask for a comparison of alternatives and for uncertainty are answered as open points, not filled.

Plant and schematic

design/v2, family HeatingAbsorptionCoolingPlant, 24 modules (read_plant).

Doublets. d0 and d1, each 2500 m deep, 300 m3/h nominal flow (range 50–300 m3/h), 0.244 m well diameter, 1000 m drainage radius, ESP with 0.7 pump efficiency, 5 bar minimum intake pressure, 0.1875 maximum gas fraction; injection well with 0.0 skin. Brine reaches the sandface at 87.0 degC and the wellhead at 84.53–87.0 degC (read_result d0.reservoir, d0.well).
Brine train. Production mixers → degasser (nominal pressure drop 14900.0 Pa, separation efficiency 1.0) → hot filter (1600.0 Pa nominal, 44.20333333333333 kg/s nominal) → heat exchanger (1500000.0 W/K, counterflow) → injection pump (600 m3/h nominal, 15.74 bar nominal head, 30 bar pressure setpoint) → injection splitters → d0/d1 injectors.
Heating. The 60 degC return is heated in the heat exchanger to 76.49–80.73 degC (duty 4.687–12.36 MW, effectiveness 0.6751–0.9109), then raised to 100 degC by the 30 MW peak boiler (efficiency 0.9) and sent to the heating consumer through 100 m of 0.5 m supply pipe; the 60 degC return comes back through 100 m of return pipe.
Cooling. A 10 MW absorption chiller (thermal COP 0.7, minimum driving temperature 80.0 degC, minimum driving difference 10 K, rejection coefficient 2.2, evaporator coefficient 1.2, auxiliary power ratio 0.01) takes 100 degC water from the heating supply as its drive and makes 20 degC chilled water from the 25 degC return; it ran 365 of 365 steps with a driving temperature difference of 66.05–97.0 K. Its heat is rejected in a 40 MW dry absorption cooler (approach 5 K, 800 kg/s, 3 bar, 15 degC setpoint, specific fan power 0.02) to air at 10 degC.
Gas. The degasser strips 0.0402–0.1287 kg/s of gas; it goes to gas_makeup, is blended with grid gas and burned in the boiler. The flare ran 0 days (0.0 MWh), so no gas was flared and methane emitted is 0.0 kt.
Electricity. Grid supply to the ESPs, the injection pump, the chiller auxiliaries and the cooler fans.
