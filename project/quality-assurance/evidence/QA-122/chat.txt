How the geothermal heat drives the cooling

Brine from the two doublets passes the degasser and the hot filter and enters heat_exchanger.hot_in at 84.65454369614213 degC, leaving hot_out at 67.10953139947935 degC; the exchanger duty is mean 7.848814505074291 MW, max 12.364068533338875 MW, min 4.687224004602223 MW (result-419c73e82e835df9, result-3c64d2361b6d3c5a, result-e6c27a4be3ab9514). On the cold side the 60 degC network return is heated to 77.54501229666278 degC (result-768196f58ec7f221). That drive loop goes to peak_boiler.fluid_in, and the boiler tops it up to the case's 100 degC heating supply: boiler_heat 76829.96207840637 MWh/yr, boiler peak 17.635931466661127 MW on 30 MW installed. drive_splitter then sends branch2 to absorption_chiller.generator_in at 100.0 degC constant (min = max = mean), mean 36.12004371819961 MW, max 133.26785714285714 MW (result-c4a1dd377995cee2). The chiller runs at cop 0.7000000000000001 constant and delivers cold_delivered 23742.528 MWh/yr with peak_cold 10.0 MW at 20 degC supply. Rejection goes to absorption_cooler: cooling_out.temperature mean 16.958950910388353 degC, cooler peak 24.38571428571427 MW of 40 MW, cooler power_in mean 0.132186873111546 MW, max 0.48771428571428543 MW (result-c4a1dd377995cee2).

In words: the 100 degC pressurised drive water is the heat source that boils the refrigerant off the absorbent in the generator; the condenser/absorber heat is rejected to the dry cooler; the evaporator produces the 20 degC chilled water. The geothermal heat — not a compressor — does the cooling work, and the only electric parasitics are small: chiller auxiliary_power_ratio 0.01 and cooler specific_fan_power 0.02 (read_plant result-9f9a330807f76a5f). The drive water leaves the generator at mean 76.00000000000011 degC (min 60.0 degC) and mixes back into the 60 degC return (result-c4a1dd377995cee2).
