Figure 1.1 - Key figures of the design
The design delivers 111.7 GWh of heat and 23.74 GWh of cold a year. The levelised cost is
49.28 EUR/MWh. Geothermal, with any heat-pump electricity, covers 39 % of the heating
demand. The largest rated units are Absorption cooler 40 MW, Peak boiler 30 MW,
Absorption chiller 10 MW. It emits 20.96 kt of CO2 over the run. All 15 assessed checks
pass.

Table 8.1 - Constraint checks of the design
No check fails. Every check could be assessed.
The checks are the 15 rules of the case. They bound brine cooling, count, production flow,
pump efficiency, reinjection ratio, temperature and unmet share on the cooling supply,
doublet and heating supply. The engine can also bound approach, co2, cold output, cop,

temperature, pump power, unmet and wellhead pressure; the case sets no rule on them, so
they are not checked. Every check judges the run of 365 d only: nothing that develops over a
longer life of the plant is checked.
