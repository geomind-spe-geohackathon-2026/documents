Brine train: production Mixer → degasser (separation efficiency 1.0) → desander (separation efficiency 0.9, waste to waste WasteGrid) → dosing InhibitorDosing (dose 10 ppm, product concentration 0.3, chemical from chemicals ChemicalGrid) → hx HeatExchanger (thermal conductance 1500000.0 W/K, counterflow) hot side → heat_pump.source_in.
Reinjection: heat_pump.source_out → injection Splitter → d0.return_in / d1.return_in; full reinjection, no brine discharge.
Heating loop: heat_users HeatingGrid return → heat_return Mixer → hx cold side → heat_pump.sink_in → heat_pump.sink_out → boiler → loads Splitter → heat_users supply (branch 1) and chiller.generator_in (branch 2).
Heat pump: 15.0 MW, mode heating, Carnot model with carnot_fraction 0.5, minimum_source_outlet_temperature 47.0 degC, minimum_temperature_lift 10.0 K.
Boiler: 30.0 MW, efficiency 0.9, peak backup (params.backup peak).
Cooling: cool_users CoolingGrid return → chiller.chilled_in → chiller.chilled_out → cool_users supply; chiller AbsorptionUnit 10.0 MW, mode chiller, thermal_cop 0.7, minimum_driving_temperature 80.0 degC, driven by the heating supply splitter; rejection chiller.cooling_out → chiller_cooler (dry Cooler, 40.0 MW, approach 5.0 K, air from air Atmosphere) → chiller.cooling_in.
Gas: degasser.gas_out → makeup.gas_in, gas_grid → makeup.grid_in, makeup.fuel_out → boiler.fuel_in, makeup.excess_out → flare (combustion efficiency 0.98) → air.
Electricity: grid ElectricityGrid → d0, d1, heat_pump, chiller, chiller_cooler. Boiler and flare flue/loss streams → air.
Control: dispatch least_cost, outages lowest_demand.

Same case, window, step, prices and scope; the two differ in plant version/topology (v1 has a 15 MW heat pump + hx; v2 has an absorption chiller driven by a splitter, pipes, injection pump, no heat pump).

Recommendation: build A (HackathonPlant, plant version 1)
Cheaper heat and cold: LCOH 43.75 vs 47.81 EUR/MWh, LCOC 51.45 vs 56.21 EUR/MWh, LCTS 45.10 vs 49.28 EUR/MWh — A is lower on all three, on identical deliveries.
Lower running cost and emissions: OPEX 3.65 vs 4.98 MEUR/yr; CO2 13.77 vs 20.96 kt.
No fuel dependence: gas bought 0.0 MWh and boiler heat 253.9 MWh (backup only) vs 65189.5 MWh gas and 76829.96 MWh boiler heat — B's heat is ~2/3 fossil, which is what drives its CO2 and opex.
Trade-off: A needs 34.64 MEUR investment vs 23.96 MEUR for B (+10.68 MEUR, a difference read off the two gemini values), and buys more grid electricity (26727.92 vs 9134.32 MWh) because the heat pump runs on power. So A is the lower-cost, lower-carbon plant but the more capital-hungry and more grid-exposed one; B is cheaper to build but dearer to run and gas-based.

Caveats: no limit exists in the case economics or the challenge requirements PDF for LCOH, LCOC, CAPEX, OPEX or CO2, so this is a comparison between the two designs, not against a limit.
